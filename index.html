cookie consent & gaze participation agreement

This experiment treats the cookie banner not as a disposable pop-up but as the actual entrance to the work. By remaining on this page you agree that your gaze, micro-movements, and approximate location may be logged on a local Raspberry Pi and used purely for research and artistic reflection. In order to proceed, this notice has to be visually traversed from top to bottom.

While you read, the system quietly records a few things: how long the session lasts, which region of the world you connect from, and a small path tracing how your attention drifts across the screen. No legal name, login, or account is required; your presence is registered only as a pattern of looking, a minor fluctuation in a dataset of blinks and hesitations.

Instead of the usual scroll-wheel gesture, your face controls a proxy cursor. As the cursor sweeps across the letters, they light up in response, slowly filling a badge at the bottom of the window. The badge becomes a proof of having stayed with this text long enough for the system to believe you have read it.

No advertising network is contacted; no data leaves the Raspberry Pi; nothing is sold. Yet the structure is still recognisable as part of the wider gaze economy in which our attention is continuously translated into value. This page simply slows that translation down and lets you dwell inside the moment when consent is usually reduced to a single impatient click.

If this arrangement feels uncomfortable, you are free to withdraw simply by closing the tab. Otherwise, once you have illuminated enough of these letters, the badge will turn fully green and the traces of your reading will be quietly grafted onto the screen, alongside those who came before.

Gently move your head so the cursor washes across the text. Each lit letter fills the NEXT → bar. Blink to invert the colours.
